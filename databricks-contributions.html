05 / WEEKEND COHORT

Taught 15 students, every weekend, for 4 months

Taught a group of 15 students for 4 months, meeting every weekend, on how to use Databricks — working through an end-to-end project from data to a finished pipeline.

These students had just come to the US, where the job market is fiercely competitive. Every weekend, I went to the university and taught them Databricks and the fundamentals of data engineering — voluntarily, while working full-time — because I wanted to give back to the community.
