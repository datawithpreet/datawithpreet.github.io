The Experience

At the Data + AI Summit in San Francisco, Databricks User Group organizers from around the world met at the User Groups Kiosk — in front of the Databricks User Groups Around the World wall, which maps 50 countries and 6K+ members.

Preet, organizer of the Boston Databricks User Group, met fellow organizers there — and Boston is on the wall itself, in the USA / Canada city list behind the group.
