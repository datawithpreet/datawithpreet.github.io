Every data talk used to start with the same architecture slide.

Ingestion on the left, lakehouse in the middle, BI and AI on the right. Correct, and useless the moment someone asked where their actual systems go.

Databricks just published a free interactive reference architecture board for each of 70 industries across 11 sectors.

Each board shows the sources, formats, teams, and use cases that industry actually runs. Every box opens a panel explaining what it is and who uses it.

The healthcare board has EHR, imaging, and revenue cycle sources. HL7 v2, FHIR, and DICOM formats. Gold holds length of stay and patient outcomes.

My favourite part is the region picker. Pick a region and the board dims every product that region cannot run, with each product's release stage listed. Filter to GA-only and you have an architecture you can defend in a review.

Any board exports to PowerPoint, PDF, or PNG. Real shapes, so you can move the boxes.

I have redrawn this slide too many times from scratch. Starting from a board that already knows my industry's systems is a better use of the hour.

When was the last time your architecture diagram matched what you actually run in production?
