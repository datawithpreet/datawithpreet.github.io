Healthcare data can feel overwhelming at first.

There are claims, enrollment, clinical data, diagnosis codes, procedure codes, risk adjustment, quality measures, surveys and many other pieces.

This series is my attempt to break that ecosystem down from a data engineering perspective — one concept at a time.

More articles are published through my [Substack](https://open.substack.com/pub/masterdataengineering).
