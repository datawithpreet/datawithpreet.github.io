Your agents' best habits are now portable.

Databricks just made Unity Catalog agent skills shareable via OpenSharing and Databricks Marketplace. A skill is now a shareable asset, same as a table or a model.

- A provider adds a skill to a share or a Marketplace listing, and a Databricks recipient uses it.

- Shared skills are read-only. The recipient gets the name, comment, and contents exactly as the provider defined them.

- Only finalized skills in managed catalogs can be shared, and only to Databricks recipients. Open recipients and clean rooms are out.

- Some accounts need an admin to switch on the Agent Skill Sharing preview first, and both sides need the Enhanced Unity AI Gateway preview, still in Beta.

Sharing used to mean data. Now it means know-how too. A team with a domain-expert skill, one that knows your claims or clinical data, can publish that behavior to a partner the same way it shares a table.

Worth trying if your agents do good work in one workspace and you want that behavior somewhere else, without handing over the keys.
