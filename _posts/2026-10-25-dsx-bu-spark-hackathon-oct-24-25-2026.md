On Oct 24–25, 2026, I took part in BU Spark! DS+X at Boston University's Center for Computing & Data Sciences, 665 Commonwealth Ave, Boston, MA 02215, in person.

## My role

- **Saturday, Oct 24 — Mentor:** I mentored teams all day, going table to table, helping them shape their ideas and their builds.
- **Sunday, Oct 25 — Technical Judge, Health & BioTech:** I judged Health & BioTech projects as a Technical Judge, reviewing their Kiro code, their GitHub repositories, and their machine learning models, and scoring them on the official rubric.

## How judging worked

Presentations ran 11:30 AM–1:30 PM on Sunday, with no live Q&A — questions went in Discord, in the thread for that project. Scoring was on six criteria: Idea Impact & Relevance (25%), Data Science Application (25%), Technical Implementation (20%), Design (10%), Responsibility & Ethics (10%), and Presentation (10%), each scored 1–5.
