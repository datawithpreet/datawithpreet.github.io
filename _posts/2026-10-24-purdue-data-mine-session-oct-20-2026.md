On Oct 20, I had the opportunity to talk with around 70 undergraduate students at Purdue Data Mine, with Professor Mark Ward.

The room had students from coding backgrounds and students from non-coding backgrounds, and that mix made the conversation better.

I talked about my daily work, my campus to corporate journey, and the things I wish I had learned in my masters that would have helped me more in my professional journey. I also talked about the challenges that come after graduating, and how to overcome them, step by step, without waiting to feel fully ready.

What I like most about Professor Ward is how much he cares about his students. He moved his office into the student residence hall at Hillenbrand Hall, so he is right there with them. He hosts dinners for his classes, he encourages students, and he pushes them to do more. He is a lovely, genuine, selfless professor. Professors like that are very rare.

Thank you, Professor Ward, for trusting me with your students. I would be glad to help again.
