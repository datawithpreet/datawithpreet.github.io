Every team has the same argument. What does "active customer" actually mean?

Databricks Pages just went generally available. A Page is a governed definition of a business concept. One term, one owner, one agreed meaning, organized by domain on the Discover page.

Curators can certify a Page as the trusted definition, or deprecate it when it goes stale. A new MANAGE PAGES permission controls who can publish and manage them. You can write Pages by hand, or generate them with Genie Code from your existing docs.

The part that matters: Pages are the modeled knowledge inside the Genie Ontology. When Genie One answers a question about a concept you defined, it uses your Page's definition first, and cites the Page so anyone can check the source.

I work with healthcare data every day. Terms like "eligible member" or "attributed patient" drift across teams and reports, and a glossary nobody reads never fixed that. A glossary the AI itself quotes back might.
