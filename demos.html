Personalized Care Management Agent

From patient data chaos to a ready-to-review care plan.

Five small agents, one job each, working one patient at a time: Auth verifies login, Triage scores risk, Channel picks email / SMS / a call script, Care Plan builds the plan, and Message renders it. The story is a before/after — one generic blast email ("Get yourself checked. Visit a physician.") versus a personalized, risk-triaged care plan plus a tailored outreach message for every patient. Pure Python, zero dependencies, fully deterministic.

BEFORE AIOne generic blast email to every patient: "Get yourself checked. Visit a physician." — no name, no condition, no next step.
AFTER AIA personalized care plan + tailored outreach message per patient: risk-triaged, condition-aware, delivered on the channel they prefer.

HHV Copilot — An Agentic AI System for Medicare Advantage In-Home Assessments

Flagship demo: the full in-home assessment lifecycle, orchestrated end to end.

The flagship demo. In-home health visits for Medicare Advantage run on manual lists, phone tag, paper notes, and spreadsheets — members slip through, care gaps stay open, and the data gets reconciled by hand before CMS submission. HHV Copilot orchestrates the whole lifecycle as agents: member identification → outreach and scheduling → assessment interpretation → care gap resolution → data validation and reconciliation → an outcome dashboard showing expected CMS revenue versus cost.

BEFORE AIManual member lists, phone-tag scheduling, paper assessments, care gaps that stay open — and a hand-built spreadsheet standing between the program and its CMS revenue.
AFTER AIOne orchestrator moves each member from identification to the outcome dashboard: prioritized outreach, interpreted assessments, closed care gaps, reconciled data — health outcomes and CMS revenue, side by side.

Restaurant Location Agent

Where should I open a Chinese restaurant?

You want to open a Chinese restaurant — but where? This agent takes Yelp-style data — ratings, review counts, price bands, cuisine mix, hours — and scores neighborhoods against real business rules: too many competitors too close, rent vs. expected demand, cuisine gaps on the street. It answers one question: which location gives your restaurant the best shot at surviving year one?

BEFORE AIWeeks of gut feel: scrolling listings, driving around at dinner time, guessing where the customers are.
AFTER AIThe agent scores every candidate block on competition, demand, and rent — and tells you where to sign the lease.

AeroYield AI — An Agentic AI Revenue Management System for Airlines

Fixed vs. rule-based vs. agentic pricing on SFO → BOS.

Airline-side pricing that watches the market. This agent runs revenue management on the SFO → BOS corridor: demand, competitor, and market intelligence feeding a demand-forecast pricing engine. The demo's centerpiece is a three-way baseline comparison — fixed tariffs vs. rule-based pricing vs. agentic pricing — so you can see exactly what the agent's decisions buy you, fare by fare.

BEFORE AIFixed tariffs and static rules: seats sell too cheap on hot days and sit empty when demand dips — the market moves, the price doesn't.
AFTER AIThe agent reads demand and competitor moves and prices the corridor continuously — with fixed and rule-based baselines side by side to prove the difference.

FixMate AI — The Intelligent Equipment Maintenance Agent

Diagnose faster. Fix smarter. Keep every store running.

BrewPoint Retail is a fictional convenience-store chain — and its coffee machines keep failing during the morning rush. FixMate AI takes it from the error code: it searches the internal manuals first, falls back to the web only when the manuals don't cover it, and walks the store employee through the fix. The demo runs three scenarios — including a knowledge-gap improvement loop, where every gap the agent hits becomes a new manual entry the next store inherits.

BEFORE AIThe store manager sits on hold with the maintenance hotline, waits two days for a technician, and learns the fix was in the manual all along — while the knowledge stays locked in technicians' heads.
AFTER AIThe agent reads the error code and solves it from the manual in minutes — and when the manual doesn't cover it, the web fallback is logged as a knowledge gap, written back into the manual for the next store.

07

GameGuard AI — An Agentic AI System for Gaming Safety and Fraud Detection

Detect harmful behavior. Investigate the context. Protect the community.

BattleSphere is a fictional online gaming company — and its chat never sleeps: trades, trash talk, team strategy, scams. Keyword filters flag "free" in a friendly giveaway while a real scammer slides by in slang, emoji, and a second language. GameGuard AI reads the full context across conversations — emoji, slang, multilingual patterns — and then investigates before it acts: evidence, history, confidence. It never auto-punishes. The demo runs three scenarios: the account scam case GG-1042, pattern-over-time escalation of a repeat offender, and a false positive it correctly lets go.

BEFORE AIKeyword filters + volunteer mods: real scams slip through written in slang and emoji, false positives punish innocent players, and banned scammers are back tomorrow on a new account.
AFTER AIThe agent reads context across conversations — emoji, slang, multilingual patterns — and every alert carries evidence, history, and confidence. It never auto-punishes: false positives are dismissed, repeat offenders escalated.

08

GuestFirst AI — An Agentic AI System for Personalized Restaurant Experiences

When satisfaction dips, find out why — fix it — and prove it's fixed.

TastePoint is a fictional restaurant chain — and lunch satisfaction has started slipping. The surveys say "too slow," but nobody can prove why. GuestFirst AI does the manager's whole job: it investigates the why — cross-referencing guest feedback with order records and kitchen operations to pin the root cause — then determines the action, coordinates the response, and verifies the resolution actually worked. The live scenario runs the full loop: lunch-delay complaints → root-cause investigation → recommendation — then the twist: one kitchen station goes down mid-demo, and the agent revises its recommendation on the fly before running a verification simulation.

BEFORE AISurveys say "too slow" — and that's all anybody knows. The manager guesses, tries a fix, and never measures whether it worked.
AFTER AIThe agent traces complaints to their root cause — feedback → order records → operations — prescribes a fix, coordinates it, and verifies the resolution. Closed-loop service recovery, with evidence.
