Site guide

Every page on this site, in one place.

A detailed summary of each page on datawithpreet.github.io — what it is about, what you will find there, and how to get to it. Start at the home page, or jump straight to any section below.

Main pages

Home

Preet Mehta — data engineer for high-stakes data. The front door of the site: who he is, selected work, skills, experience across EXL Service, CVS Health, LinkedIn, Searce and DCM Shriram, writing, community work, and how to contact him.

Events & Experiences

A rotating showcase of upcoming events, followed by filterable cards for every hackathon, conference, workshop and university session — judging, mentoring, speaking and community work, each with its own detail page (see the Events section below).

Writing

Ideas, systems & lessons from the field. Practical writing on data engineering, healthcare analytics, cloud platforms and AI/ML — the full catalogue of posts lives under /writing/ (see the Writing section below).

Book Series: Fundamentals of Data Engineering

One post a day from Fundamentals of Data Engineering by Joe Reis and Matt Housley — each chapter explained in plain language, from what data engineering actually is to data architecture principles (17 chapters published so far).

Daily

Daily notes from the data world. One short post every day — Databricks updates, data engineering notes, and lessons from healthcare data work.

Research & Publications

Research that connects data, AI, and real-world impact — research interests at the intersection of healthcare, AI and data, publications, and peer review service.

Resources

Learn something. Build something. Data engineering books, resume templates, videos and interview questions curated by Preet — plus the free healthcare data projects for students.

AI Demos

AI agents that do the work. Nine hands-on agentic AI demos, including a personalized care management agent, the flagship HHV Copilot, a restaurant location agent, AeroYield, a music mood agent, FixMate, GameGuard, GuestFirst and a resume reviewer — each with a full project deep dive.

Contributions

Judging, mentoring, teaching and speaking at hackathons, universities and communities — the full record of Preet's community contributions.

Databricks Contributions

Preet's contributions to the Databricks community — certifications, hackathons, teaching, and the Boston Databricks User Group he organizes, plus the daily Databricks posts.

Free Healthcare Data Projects

Three free, hands-on healthcare data engineering projects for students — starting with designing an EHR data model and taking a pipeline to production — built for the NYU healthcare projects giveaway.

Resume Reviewer AI

An interactive tool: upload a resume (PDF/DOCX) and a job description, and get feedback against 33 hiring-manager rules plus job-description keyword matching.
